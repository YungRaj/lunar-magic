# Lunar Magic 3.63 graphics-compression runtime resource family

This clean-room observation binds the complete runtime family selected by the authenticated
Lunar Magic 3.63 executable. The executable itself is not redistributed.

- `Lunar Magic.exe` SHA-256:
  `b64998b637e553c9adb96dd893140b5b8d0303c7a0f46a1fdab5f887a1d46eff`.
- Ghidra `InstallGraphicsCompressionMode1Runtime` at `$00480120` selects PE resource type `$1F4`,
  ID `$1FE` for ordinary LoROM, `$200` for ExLoROM, or `$203` for SA-1/mapper-feature state.
- Ghidra `InstallGraphicsCompressionMode2Runtime` at `$00480220` selects the corresponding IDs
  `$1FF`, `$201`, or `$204`.
- `ConvertRomGraphicsCompressionMode` at `$00480320` is the sole current-editor dispatcher and
  passes modes 1 and 2 to those installers after removing the prior runtime.

The ExLoROM resources are not aliases for their LoROM counterparts: the mode-1 payload differs at
offsets `$DC` and `$108`, and mode 2 differs at `$153` and `$1E9`, changing fixed mapping operands
from `$0000` to `$3000`. The SA-1 resources are structurally larger and use `$7F:C020`-family RAM
operands. Rust previously installed the ordinary LoROM mode-1 resource for every mapper and the
ordinary LoROM mode-2 resource for ExLoROM, while its detector expected a different SA-1 owner.

`all_six_lunar_magic_363_runtime_resources_are_embedded_exactly` binds the lengths, CRCs, and
trailers. `detector_accepts_every_current_mapper_runtime_and_rejects_cross_mapper_substitution`
materializes all six mapper/mode installations through real SNES pointers and RATS owners, then
requires complete-runtime checksum rejection after corruption. Historical inputs remain a separate
compatibility inventory; they are not conflated with the six outputs generated by 3.63 itself.
`ValidateAndInitializeOpenedRom` reads the metadata mode nibble without consulting a runtime-version
table, and `ConvertRomGraphicsCompressionMode` has no historical-signature dispatcher. Thus
arbitrary hook bodies are not a finite generation product. The distinct released-editor input
found by the retained corpus—the exact `$1AF`/`LM 00 01` optimized-LZ2 generation—is authenticated
and migrated separately, including all dependent graphics, ExGFX, and event data.
